Lumora — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Lumora is an ambient screen, music and podcast app for Android TV and phones. It requires no account, shows no ads, and the developer operates no server and receives no data from the app.

Data the app processes

  • Media you point it at — your local photos and music, or files on your own network share (SMB). They are read for playback/display only and never leave your network.
  • Network share credentials — if you add an SMB share, its address and login are stored locally on your device and used only to connect to your own server.
  • Offline downloads — podcast episodes, videos and music you choose to download are stored on your device only.
  • Internet radio and podcasts — streams and RSS feeds are fetched directly from their providers (the public radio-browser directory; the Apple Podcasts public charts and artwork for the "Top of the country" list); those third parties see your IP address like any web request.
  • Photo frame (phone app, optional) — your photos are shown from your device or network share. If a photo carries GPS coordinates, they are read on the device to show a place name and a small map: the place name comes from Android's system geocoder (provided by Google or your device maker, see their policies), and map tiles are fetched from OpenStreetMap, which sees the map area requested and your IP address. The weather is fetched from Open-Meteo using the coordinates of the place you choose or of the photo; city search queries are sent to Open-Meteo. News headlines are fetched directly from the public RSS feeds of the publishers you select (e.g. franceinfo, Le Monde, RTS, BBC). None of this goes through the developer, and you can leave these options off.
  • Settings — kept locally on the device.
  • Microphone and camera — Lumora no longer uses them: neither the phone nor the TV app requests the microphone or camera permission.

What is never done

  • No data is collected, transmitted to, or stored by the developer.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no tracking SDKs, no account.

Multi-device control (local network)

Lumora devices can discover and control each other on YOUR local network (e.g. the phone app sending a radio to the TV). Nothing goes through the internet.

Purchases

The one-time unlock is processed entirely by Google Play Billing; the app only stores the resulting yes/no entitlement locally.

Data retention and deletion

Uninstalling the app removes all its local data.

Children

Lumora is not directed at children under 13.
