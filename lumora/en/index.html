Lumora · Android

Podcasts, radio, music and photos. On your phone or on the TV.

Lumora brings your podcasts, radio from around the world, your music, photos and movies into one app. The phone is the remote, the TV is the screen.

Podcasts

  • Catalog by topic and by publisher, search, favorites
  • Latest episodes from all your podcasts, resume where you left off

Radio, music, photos, movies

  • Thousands of live stations, ad-free filter

On the TV

  • Google TV app, or cast to a Chromecast

  • The phone becomes the remote, or a screen itself

FAQ

Is Lumora free?

Yes, no ads and no account. A one-time purchase unlocks advanced options.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. No data collected, no tracking. Everything stays on your devices and local network.
