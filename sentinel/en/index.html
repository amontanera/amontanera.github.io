Sentinel Dashcam · Android

Your phone becomes a dash cam. Your old phone, a security camera.

Sentinel Dashcam is a free Android app. On the road it loop-records and keeps sudden moves. At home it recognizes a person, an animal or a vehicle and alerts you. Everything stays on the phone.

Get it on Google Play

Screenshots

Dash cam

  • Loop recording: oldest segments are recycled, storage never fills up
  • Hard braking, impact, pothole: the clip is kept automatically
  • Date, time, GPS, speed and g-force burned into the video
  • Starts on its own when the phone connects to the car's Bluetooth, stops on arrival
  • Car and Motorbike profiles, stabilization, black screen mode

Security camera

  • Reuse an old phone sleeping in a drawer
  • Smart detection: person, animal, vehicle. No alerts for shadows or rain
  • Sound alerts: baby crying, breaking glass, fire alarm, barking
  • Analyzed on the phone, even offline

Sentinel Pro, one-time purchase

  • Road and cabin recorded at the same time
  • All your cameras live on the TV (Google TV, Chromecast)
  • Remote access from another phone, QR code pairing
  • Automatic copy to your NAS, WebDAV or cloud folder

FAQ

Is Sentinel free?

Yes. Dash cam, surveillance and detection are free, with no ads. Sentinel Pro is a one-time purchase, no subscription.

Do I need an account or a cloud plan?

No. No account, no server. Videos stay on the phone, or go only where you send them (NAS, WebDAV, cloud folder).

Will an old phone work?

Yes, Sentinel runs on Android 8 and later. An old phone on a charger makes a great security camera.

Is a dash cam legal?

Rules vary by country, especially for audio and sharing footage. Check your local law.
